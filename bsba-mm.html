About the Program

The Bachelor of Science in Business Administration major in Marketing Management prepares students for professional careers involving marketing strategies, customer relations, sales, advertising, promotions, and business development.

The program develops students' communication, analytical, creative, and managerial abilities while providing practical knowledge that can be applied in modern business environments.

Program Highlights

Marketing Strategy

Learn how businesses develop marketing strategies to reach customers and achieve organizational goals.

Consumer Behavior

Understand customer needs, preferences, buying behavior, and factors affecting purchasing decisions.

Business Communication

Develop communication, presentation, and promotional skills for professional business environments.
