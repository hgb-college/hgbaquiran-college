About HGBaquiran College

Developing Competent and Future-Ready Graduates

HGBaquiran College is committed to providing a quality and learner-centered institution that develops competent and future-ready graduates.


The institution promotes relevant research and innovation that contribute to knowledge and societal development, while engaging in responsive community service and partnership that improve lives and strengthen communities.

A Learner-Centered Institution

HGBaquiran College's commitment centers on quality education, research and innovation, and meaningful service to communities.

Our Direction

Mission & Vision


Mission

HGBaquiran College is committed to:

  • Provide Quality and Learner-centered Institution that develops competent and future-ready graduates.
  • Promote Relevant Research and Innovation that contribute to knowledge and societal development; and
  • Engage in Responsive Community Service and Partnership that improve lives and strengthen communities.

Vision

HGBaquiran College is one of the leading private institutions in the Cagayan Valley Region with growing global recognition for Excellence, Innovation, and Transformation service.
