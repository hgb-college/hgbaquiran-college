Bachelor of Science in Industrial Security Management

Develop knowledge and professional skills in security management, risk awareness, safety, security operations, and organizational protection.

About the Program

The Bachelor of Science in Industrial Security Management prepares students for professional pathways related to security management, safety, risk awareness, and the protection of people, property, facilities, and organizations.

The program develops students' knowledge of security principles, organizational safety, security operations, risk management, and professional communication.

Career Opportunities

Graduates may pursue opportunities in private security, corporate security, safety and risk management, facility protection, and related fields, subject to applicable qualifications and regulations.

  • Corporate Security
  • Security Operations
  • Safety and Risk Support
  • Facility Security
  • Loss Prevention Support
  • Security Administration
  • Security Program Support
