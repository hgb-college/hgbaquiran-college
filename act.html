About the Program

Associate in Computer Technology

The Associate in Computer Technology (ACT) is a technology-focused undergraduate program designed to develop foundational knowledge and practical skills in computing and information technology.

The program introduces students to computer systems, software applications, programming, networking, and other fundamental areas of computer technology.
