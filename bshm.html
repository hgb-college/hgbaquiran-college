About the Program

Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management

The Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management (BSHM) is an undergraduate program designed to prepare students for professional careers in the hospitality and service industry.

The program develops knowledge, skills, and professional values needed in areas such as hotel operations, food and beverage service, tourism, events, and other hospitality-related fields.
